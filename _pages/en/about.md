## tl;dr
I build robots that understand human intent and respond with intelligent, intuitive behavior -- blending human-robot interaction and robot learning to provide assistance that is adaptive and safe. My expertise spans reinforcement learning, multimodal perception, and language-driven control, and I specialize in developing full-stack systems that integrate interaction, perception, learning, and control into robots deployable in real-world user studies.

## about me
I’m a final-year PhD candidate at Nanyang Technological University, advised by Prof. Ang Wei Tech. My research lies at the intersection of human-robot interaction and robot learning, focusing on how robots can better understand human intent and behavior, and respond in ways that are intuitive, safe, and continually improving through real-world interaction.

My technical expertise includes reinforcement learning, multimodal perception, and language-driven control, with hands-on experience in sim-to-real transfer and real-world deployment in user trials. I aim to bridge research and application by turning embodied AI models into deployable robots that move beyond the lab and into the real world.

### current projects:
- **Embodied AI Data Factory**: Leading efforts to create high-fidelity datasets for dexterous and bimanual manipulation. Developing an intuitive teleoperation system to reduce operator burden and accelerate real robot data collection, enabling the training of robust, generalizable foundation-model policies. 

- **Robot-Assisted Feeding**: Leading the development of an end-to-end system deployed in elderly user trials. Integrating imitation learning, reinforcement learning, multimodal perception and language-driven adaptation to personalize the mealtime assistance in a safe and reliable manner.

When I'm not working on robots, I enjoy creative, hands-on work — crocheting, knitting, 3D printing and tinkering. I value design that is both aesthetic and purposeful, and believe the best solutions are not only technically sound, but also elegant, intuitive, and human-centered.
